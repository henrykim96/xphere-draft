Participate

Three ways into the network.

Stake

Stake XP

Deposit native XP in the Union Vault. Rewards come from what the Foundation's validator node actually earns. 0% platform fees, 7-day cooldown, no node required.

How staking works →
Mine

Mine the Proof Chain

The Proof Chain is secured by xpHash proof of work. Since the xpHash hard fork, blocks are sealed with an IceRiver XP0 ASIC. Rewards pay straight to your targetMiner address, with no claim transaction.

Mining guide ↗
Union

Join the Union

Union members validate Proof Chain blocks, record them on the Main Chain and vote on network policy. Each slot requires a 35,000,000 XP stake, dedicated infrastructure and Foundation approval. Interim Foundation nodes burn what they earn, so a new member displaces a burn, not another member.
