Overview

What is
XPHERE Union?

XPHERE Union is the core group of validators within the network.

Enhances network stability, scalability, and ecosystem growth.

Responsible for block creation and validation based on a dual-chain architecture.

Slots are capped at 31 and emission declines 26.28% per year — earlier members hold a larger share for longer.

Every 60 blocks

A reward is minted every 60 blocks and shared across active slots.

−26.28% a year

Emission reduces 26.28% each year, so the earliest years carry the largest block rewards.

4 interim nodes

The Foundation currently operates 4 interim validator nodes so the council stays full. Each is retired the moment a new Union member takes its place, and everything those nodes earn in the meantime is burned — a new member displaces a burn, not another member's share.
